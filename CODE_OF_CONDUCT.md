# Code of conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be considerate,
welcome constructive disagreement, assume good intent, and focus feedback on
technical decisions rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sharing
private information, sustained disruption, and other conduct inappropriate for
a professional open-source community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates these standards.
Report abuse privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile or
use GitHub's built-in abuse-reporting tools. Do not use public issues to share
private information.

Enforcement decisions will prioritize the safety and privacy of affected
participants and will be handled as consistently as practical.
